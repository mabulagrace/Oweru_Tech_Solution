How Oweru Tech Solutions collects, uses and protects your information.

1. Who we are

Oweru Tech Solutions (Oweru International Ltd) designs, builds and maintains software systems. When you use our website, request a quote or order a service, we handle the personal information you provide.

2. Information we collect

  • Details you give us in an enquiry: name, email, phone, business name, country, address and a description of what you need.
  • When you request a website health check, we record only the public web addresses you ask us to scan. We never log into any site and never collect personal data from third-party websites.
  • Basic technical data such as the date and time of your visit and the pages you view.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare quotes.
  • To communicate about your project, payments and support.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and project records for as long as needed to deliver and support your services and to meet our legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your personal information at any time, except where we are required to keep records by law.

5. Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us at hello@oweru.tech.

6. Security

We store data on secure servers, restrict access to authorised staff only, and use encrypted connections (HTTPS) on this website.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version always appears on this page with the date it was last updated.

8. Contact

For any privacy question or request, email hello@oweru.tech or call +255 123 456 789.
